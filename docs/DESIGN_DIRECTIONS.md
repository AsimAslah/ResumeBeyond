# Design direction comparison

## Direction A — Premium portfolio evolution

Route: `/directions/evolution`

Character-led, spacious, and expressive. Mini Byte remains visually central, serif display typography preserves editorial personality, and projects appear as a chronological gallery.

Strengths: distinctive identity, memorable character, and continuity with the original portfolio.

Tradeoff: role fit and evidence take longer to scan.

## Direction B — Recruiter-first AI resume

Route: `/` (selected)

Compact evidence hierarchy, 60-second candidate brief, three capability cards, relevance-ordered project evidence, status labels, source IDs, and persistent Mini Byte access.

Strengths: fastest role comprehension, strongest claim/evidence pairing, and clearer status/contribution boundaries.

Tradeoff: intentionally less playful than Direction A.

## Recorded decision

The Replit comparison selected Direction B while preserving Direction A as a complete production route. Subsequent refinement restored evidence destinations, mobile behavior, and browser coverage without redesigning either direction. The generated comparison sandbox was removed because it duplicated production code, assets, and dependencies; the two production routes, pull-request history, this decision record, and [REPLIT_DESIGN_EVIDENCE.md](REPLIT_DESIGN_EVIDENCE.md) preserve the comparison evidence.
