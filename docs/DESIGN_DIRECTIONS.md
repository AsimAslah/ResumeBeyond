# Design direction comparison

## Direction A - Evolution of the existing portfolio

Route: `/directions/evolution`

Character-led, spacious, and expressive. Mini Byte remains visually central, serif display typography preserves editorial personality, and projects appear as a chronological gallery.

Strengths: distinctive identity, memorable character, closer to the original portfolio.

Tradeoff: role fit and evidence take longer to scan.

## Direction B - Recruiter-first AI resume

Route: `/` (selected locally)

Compact evidence hierarchy, 60-second candidate brief, three capability cards, relevance-ordered project evidence, status labels, source IDs, and persistent Mini Byte access.

Strengths: fastest role comprehension, strongest claim/evidence pairing, clearer status and contribution boundaries.

Tradeoff: less playful than Direction A.

## Local decision

Direction B is the local default because the target role and three core capabilities are visible before project exploration, while Direction A remains available for Replit Design comparison. The final challenge decision must be reviewed in Replit Design and refined with Replit Agent before release.
